1. Introduction

Kaarivo ("we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit our website, contact us, request a quotation or enquire about our services.

Kaarivo provides home repair, property maintenance and improvement services in Kolkata, West Bengal, India. By using our website or voluntarily providing information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy, subject to applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information: Your name, telephone number, email address and other details you choose to share.
  • Service information: Property location, service address, type of repair or maintenance required, preferred appointment details and information relevant to your enquiry.
  • Communications: Messages and details you provide through telephone calls, WhatsApp or other communication channels.
  • Technical information: Information such as browser type, device details, approximate location derived from technical data, pages visited and website usage data, where made available through analytics or server logs.

Please share only information reasonably necessary to help us respond to your enquiry or provide the requested service.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected to:

  • Respond to enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Understand your property service requirements.
  • Coordinate appointments, visits and service delivery.
  • Communicate updates about a requested service.
  • Maintain website functionality and improve user experience.
  • Understand website traffic and performance.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, security incidents and unauthorised activity.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not intend to use your contact information for unrelated promotional communications without an appropriate basis or consent where required by law.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information only where reasonably necessary, including with:

  • Service professionals or contractors involved in fulfilling your specific service request.
  • Website hosting, analytics, communications and other technology providers supporting our operations.
  • Professional advisers or public authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

We aim to limit shared information to what is necessary for the relevant purpose. Third-party providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies, similar technologies and analytics tools to understand website usage, measure performance and improve functionality.

We use Google Analytics through Google tag technology to help understand how visitors interact with our website, subject to the tool's configuration and applicable requirements. This may involve technical information such as pages viewed, browser details and interaction data.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features. For additional details about data handled by Google, review Google's own privacy documentation.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should avoid sending passwords, payment credentials, government identity numbers or other highly sensitive information through ordinary website enquiries or messaging channels unless specifically required through a suitable, secure process.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable requirements, subject to legitimate retention needs.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by us, or to raise concerns about how it is handled.

You may contact us using the details below to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request before taking action. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

9. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may link to third-party websites or services, including WhatsApp. If you follow these links, the third party's own privacy policy and terms may apply.

We do not control the privacy practices or security of independent third-party services. Please review their policies before sharing personal information through them.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and property services are intended for adults arranging or enquiring about property services. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children in connection with these services. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website practices or applicable legal requirements. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact Kaarivo.
